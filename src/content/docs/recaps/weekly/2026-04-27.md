His higher-level framing is in a [Google Doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/17eexzDhYJGl2FJIsdacL8ikMzNSBV38Ji01MK6YHqWI/edit?tab=t.0).

## hinton-problems v1 shipped

Yaroslav asked Yad about the state of his agentic loop, since he wanted reference implementations for all of Hinton's learning problems and had only [encoder-4-2-4](https://github.com/cybertronai/hinton-problems/tree/main/encoder-4-2-4) done. Yad posted a minimum spec as [issue #1](https://github.com/cybertronai/hinton-problems/issues/1) and planned to deploy waves of ten implementations at a time once an unrelated release finished. By May 2 he had 33 of 53 stubs open across six wave PRs, and by May 3 hinton-problems v1 was shipped: all 53 stubs in main across ten wave PRs (#32 through #41), with a RESULTS.md catalog in PR #42 reporting 27 stubs that reproduce paper claims and 25 partial. The [site](https://cybertronai.github.io/hinton-problems/) is live. Yad also stood up a related repo for ICLR efficient-learning and matmul literature at [iclr-lit-builder](https://github.com/0bserver07/iclr-lit-builder), not yet moved.

## ByteDMD and tooling

Yaroslav noted that ByteDMD is a useful heuristic but may not actually be a lower bound, and after weeks trying to connect it to energy without success, the next step is to skip the bounding and tighten agent evaluations by forcing an explicit IR and scoring energy on Bill Dally's 2d grid model. He posted a [current-bounds summary doc](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TcRBets7L1TLF628uUe8DOoNcGGxM7rl5ECZ4mT25ks/edit?tab=t.0) and the [simplified explicit communication model](https://github.com/cybertronai/ByteDMD/blob/dev/docs/simplified-explicit-communication-model.md). He asked about alternatives to Claude Code, and Andy walked through his Nix-based setup for running multiple agents in parallel, linked from his [home-manager config](https://github.com/zh4ngx/nixos/blob/main/modules/home-manager/default.nix#L90-L109).
