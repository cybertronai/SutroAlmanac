Recaps are the periodic layer of the Almanac. Each one snapshots where the work
stands at a point in time: what moved, what is active, what is worth reusing. The
[Timeline](/timeline/) absorbs them into the long record.

There are three layers below, from coarse to fine. The monthly recaps are the
primary record, written from the sources directly. The weekly recaps zoom in on a
single week. The catch-ups are the original status notes the months were built from.
Source material is the group's Telegram archive, the shared Google Docs, the
[meeting notes](/meetings/), and the repos, with each claim traceable.

## Monthly

The primary record. Five months, February through June 2026.

- [June 2026](/recaps/2026-06/): current. Auto-research loops after the Modal
  hackathon, the structure-specific-optimizer thread, and the four challenges.
- [May 2026](/recaps/2026-05/): Yad's hinton and schmidhuber wave-build catalogs,
  the four challenges, and the wikitext launch.
- [April 2026](/recaps/2026-04/): ByteDMD adopted as the primary metric, Yad and
  Seth's floor-gap survey, and the pivot to modeling energy directly.
- [March 2026](/recaps/2026-03/): the founding month. Yad builds SutroYaro, GF(2)
  settles sparse parity, and the metric shifts to DMC.
- [February 2026](/recaps/2026-02/): formation. The energy thesis takes shape and
  the search for a fast toy problem begins.
