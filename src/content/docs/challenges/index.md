title: The challenges
description: The four energy-efficient-learning challenges the group runs in parallel.
---

The group runs four challenges at once, each in its own repo with its own leaderboard. The cost is measured as data movement, not raw accuracy.

- [Challenge #1: Sparse parity](/challenges/sparse-parity/). The original benchmark, scored under ByteDMD.
- [Challenge #2: Energy-efficient matmul](/challenges/matmul/). Minimum-energy 16x16 matmul on the Dally 2D grid.
- [Challenge #3: Sparse parity on the grid](/challenges/grid-parity/). The same task in about nine grid instructions.
- [Challenge #4: wikitext](/challenges/wikitext/). Minimum-Joules language modeling on WikiText-103.

Two baseline catalogs, [hinton-problems](https://github.com/cybertronai/hinton-problems) and [schmidhuber-problems](https://github.com/cybertronai/schmidhuber-problems), feed the process that picks the next challenge. For the fuller picture see [Projects & Initiatives](/projects/).
